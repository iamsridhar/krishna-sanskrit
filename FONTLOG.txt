FONTLOG for Krishna Sanskrit
============================

Basic font information
----------------------
Krishna Sanskrit is a Devanagari font for Vedic and Sanskrit texts. Its Devanagari is
derived from Sanskrit2020 (advaita56, Version 1.30, July 27 2022), which is based on
Sanskrit 2003 by Omkarananda Ashram Himalayas; five द-stack glyphs are derived from
Shobhika (IIT Bombay); the Latin is derived from Gentium Medium (SIL Global).
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

ChangeLog
---------
1.004 (2026-10-11)
- प्र (about 9,300 occurrences in the Taittiriya texts): the slanted ra-stroke is about a
  third longer and reaches below the bowl, so it no longer reads as a stub. Outline only:
  same glyph, advance and mark attachment; प्र्य unchanged (p25).

1.003 (2026-10-10)
- न्त्र (about 1,850 occurrences in the Taittiriya texts) is written as half न followed by
  त्र, like न्त्र्य already was, instead of upstream's fused glyph (p20).

1.002 (2026-10-09)
- Anunasika with i-matra (व्ँवि; 1,326 occurrences in the Taittiriya texts): the
  candrabindu sat on the headline under the long i-matra arc and touched it. It is now
  lifted so its bottom sits at the top of the arc, still over the first part of the
  conjunct; the svarita stays clear of it. Forms without i-matra are unchanged (p40).

1.001 (2026-10-09)
- Anunasika before y, v, l (य्ँय, व्ँव, ल्ँल; 3,341 occurrences in the Taittiriya texts):
  shown as the joined conjunct (य्य, व्व, ल्ल) with the candrabindu over its first part,
  instead of a visible virama, the candrabindu and the full letter. The text is unchanged,
  so search and copy-paste still give व्ँव. Includes the i-matra forms (व्ँवि uses the
  i-matra of व्वि). When a further conjunct follows (व्ँव्या) the rendering is unchanged.
  The two consonants are in different syllables, so this uses a new 'rlig' lookup;
  HarfBuzz and CoreText give identical results (p40, tests/test_anunasika.py).

1.000 (2026-09-30) First release.
- Vedic accents: anudatta (U+0952) hangs beneath the u, uu, vocalic r/rr, vocalic l/ll
  vowel signs and virama instead of overlapping them (about 44,800 occurrences in the
  Taittiriya corpus), via a new mark-to-mark lookup with a GDEF mark filtering set so
  reph, anusvara and svarita between the two are skipped (p10).
- Conjuncts: द्ध, द्ध्य, द्भ्य, द्व्य, द्द्य redrawn from Shobhika's outlines (open stack,
  compact joined ya-phala), fitted to Sanskrit2020's size, stem weight and headline
  (p15). ब्ज, क्त्र, क्च, च्छ्र, ञ्छ्र, न्त्र्य split into half form + letter; द्ध्व,
  द्ध्म, द्ध्र, द्घ्र, द्र्व written as द् + rest; a leftover below-base ra (र्र्ष) as
  र + virama (p20).
- i-matra: 23 longer hooks, made by stretching the flat top of the upstream arc (also
  for the anusvara, candrabindu and reph-merged forms), chosen per conjunct so the hook
  lands on the final consonant as it does on a single consonant; rules generalise by
  width class to unseen clusters (p30).
- Latin: Sanskrit2020's URW Palladio Latin (AGPL/GPL, and without the ISO-15919 Vedic
  combining accents) replaced by Gentium Medium's Latin, IPA, combining marks, Latin
  Extended A-E and Additional, punctuation and currency, scaled to Sanskrit2020's
  x-height; below-marks also attach to ꣳ/ꣴ.
- Renderer robustness: new lookups registered in psts and ZWJ-aware so CoreText matches
  HarfBuzz on every corpus cluster; upstream's NULL mark anchor fixed so GPOS passes
  the OpenType Sanitizer (p05); duplicate/nested composite components cleaned (p95).
- Release engineering: glyphs renamed to Adobe Glyph List names for the text they render
  and 1,127 upstream PUA cmap entries removed, so text copied out of PDFs is Unicode;
  ttfautohint over the whole font; USE_TYPO_METRICS with typo = hhea and a Windows clip
  box covering all ink (p90); Windows-only name records. FontBakery universal: 0 FAIL.
