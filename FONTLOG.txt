FONTLOG for Krishna Sanskrit
==========================

Basic font information
----------------------
Krishna Sanskrit is a Devanagari font for Vedic Sanskrit, derived from Sanskrit2020
(advaita56, Version 1.30, July 27 2022), which is itself based on Sanskrit 2003 by
Omkarananda Ashram Himalayas. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

ChangeLog
---------
0.100 (unreleased)
- p00: Renamed from Sanskrit2020 to Krishna Sanskrit. Upstream copyright retained.
- p10: Anudatta (U+0952) now hangs beneath the u, uu, vocalic r/rr, vocalic l/ll
  vowel signs and virama instead of overlapping them (about 44,800 occurrences
  in the Taittiriya corpus). Implemented as a new mark-to-mark lookup with a GDEF
  mark filtering set, so reph, anusvara and svarita between the two are skipped.
- p20: Cramped fused conjuncts are split into upstream component glyphs, following
  Shobhika's structure: ddhya, dbhya, dvya, ddya (and ndddhya, ndvya, bddhya,
  gddhya, rddhya) as stack + full ya; bja, ktra, cchra, nycchra as half form +
  letter; a leftover below-base ra (rrsha) as ra + virama. Implemented as a final
  multiple-substitution lookup, so marks use the components' existing anchors.
  118 corpus clusters change. Not yet addressed: ddhna (kept as upstream's d + dhna
  by choice).
- p30: The i-matra hook now reaches the final consonant of a conjunct. 23 longer
  variants (120-unit steps) of the i-matra and its anusvara/candrabindu/reph-merged
  forms are made by stretching the flat top of the upstream arc; anusvara,
  candrabindu and reph move with the tip. A chained contextual lookup picks the
  length from the width of the glyphs in front of the final consonant, learned from
  the corpus plus all generated 2- and 3-consonant clusters. 311 corpus clusters
  (16,576 tokens) change; every conjunct's hook lands within 90 units of its target.
  The i-matra before a single consonant is unchanged. Variant glyphs are unhinted.
- p20/p30 lookups moved to the psts feature so CoreText (which applies features one
  at a time) runs them after upstream's ligatures, as HarfBuzz does; the i-matra
  rules gained explicit ZWJ variants because CoreText does not skip ZWJ in contexts.
  CoreText and HarfBuzz now agree on every corpus cluster Krishna changes. Checked in
  XeLaTeX (FakeBold=0.5, Scale=1.1): all fixes render; digit + anudatta also renders
  correctly without the site's \anudattadigit workaround.

- p15: द्ध, द्ध्य, द्भ्य, द्व्य, द्द्य redrawn from Shobhika's outlines (open stack with
  a compact, joined ya-phala), scaled to Krishna's size, stems thickened toward
  Krishna's contrast, Krishna's headline. Replaces the earlier stack + full-ya split.
- p20: द्ध्व, द्ध्म, द्ध्र, द्घ्र, द्र्व written as द् + rest (reviewer choice); क्च and
  न्त्र्य split into half form + letter.
- p30: fixed the stretched i-matra for the reph/anusvara-merged forms (the stem was
  being moved); rules now generalise by width class, covering attested 4-consonant
  clusters.
- Release engineering: glyphs renamed to Adobe Glyph List names for the text they
  render and 1,127 upstream PUA cmap entries removed, so text copied out of PDFs is
  Unicode (it was PUA/garbage with Sanskrit2020); ttfautohint over the whole font;
  p05 fixes an upstream NULL mark anchor that made OTS reject GPOS; p90 vertical
  metrics (USE_TYPO_METRICS, no clipping of tall Vedic stacks on Windows); p95
  composite clean-up; legacy Mac name records dropped. FontBakery universal: 0 FAIL.
  Devanagari-only WOFF2 web subset + CSS.
